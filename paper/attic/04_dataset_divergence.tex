\paragraph{Primary supervision: public per-token traces.}
Model development uses \texttt{herald-logits}, a public corpus of per-token
records from compressed generations of Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct across six
KV-cache compressors (ExpectedAttention, KNorm, Random, SnapKV, StreamingLLM,
TOVA) and seven compression ratios on four tasks (GSM8K, HumanEval, IFEval,
LongBench). Each record carries
zero-cost statistics of the next-token distribution at that step together
with the oracle divergence fields of Section~\ref{sec:target}, computed
against the uncompressed model on the same realized prefix. The corpus
comprises approximately \todo{6.9M tokens across 29{,}240 runs; re-verify
against the dataset card}. Uncompressed baseline runs appear with an inactive
compressor and ratio zero, giving the forecaster genuine zero-target support.

\paragraph{Grouping structure.}
Token rows are not independent samples: rows within one generation share a
trajectory, and generations from one prompt share content. All splitting and
resampling keeps every run and every token of a prompt in a single fold, and
prompts receive equal total weight regardless of how many tokens they
contribute.
