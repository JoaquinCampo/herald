# Strategic retreat after VT and MuSiQue pilots

## Decision

Pause new collection. There is no validated predictor and no confirmation
population. The current evidence does not justify choosing another prompt,
horizon, or compression severity from exposed outcomes, and it does not justify
refitting a closed NIAH feature family.

The failure is specific, not a claim that signed task-quality risk is
impossible. The target is identifiable only when the paired action changes
final score with enough variation, the reference is competent enough to make
that change meaningful, and an admissible decision-time observable can measure
the susceptibility before the action.

## What the existing evidence establishes

- **Action severity:** The exposed 051 matrix shows stable prompt-specific
  susceptibility across Knorm .05, .10 and .20 on 12 NIAH prompts. Its 50%
  privileged gain uses the same prompt's other action outcomes, so it proves
  repeatability of an action-response surface, not an admissible predictor.
  Severity alone is therefore not a sufficient reason to tune another rate.
- **Task reference quality:** VT has 16 perfect references and 16 perfect
  actions, so every loss is zero. MuSiQue has mean reference F1 .4215, only
  5/16 references at F1 >= .8, and EA.10 loss zero on every row. These slices
  cannot support a risk predictor, for opposite reasons, and are closed by
  062 without tuning their exposed outcomes.
- **Decision-time information:** Head retention, generic digit likelihood,
  value-level retention, future-query attention error, and related B0 NIAH
  families are closed by 041, 045/046 and 047/048. The future-query result is
  privileged, and the 053 literature review found no direct primary method
  for this estimand. No surviving observable has prospective evidence.

## Competing hypotheses

1. **The action response is real but the decision clock is wrong.** Prompt
   susceptibility may be stable across rates, while useful information appears
   only after future queries or generated state. Expected: privileged diagnostics
   remain predictive while every B0-only proxy fails. Learning: a new study
   would need an explicitly changed decision boundary, not another B0 feature.

2. **Outcome populations are the main bottleneck.** Binary NIAH losses can
   saturate, VT has no loss variation, and MuSiQue has inadequate references.
   Expected: a competent graded task with both positive and nonpositive signed
   losses is necessary before predictor information can be tested. Learning:
   outcome viability is a prerequisite, not evidence of predictability.

3. **No cheap admissible signal is stable across tasks and actions.** The
   privileged cross-rate result can coexist with failure of every pre-action
   observable tested so far. Expected: another proxy within the closed families
   yields another negative result. Learning: the project may need a different
   compressor or task-quality substrate rather than more feature work.

4. **A joint task/action model is required.** Prompt susceptibility may be
   meaningful only conditional on compressor, budget and task mechanics.
   Expected: a future fresh design needs action-aware baselines and must model
   one predeclared action schedule jointly. Learning: selecting a favorable
   fixed rate would answer a narrower question and repeat the current trap.

## One bounded next step: existing-artifact eligibility audit

Perform one read-only audit over the frozen VT, MuSiQue, NIAH and generic
numeric records. Do not collect data, run a model, refit a predictor, inspect
new outcomes, or choose a prompt, horizon, severity, subgroup or feature.
Produce one table with, for each frozen slice: complete-row count, reference
quality distribution, action-quality distribution, signed-loss support, action
identity and rate, independent-state status, and the status of every candidate
observable as admissible pre-action, privileged/future, or outcome-derived.

The audit passes only if it identifies an already exposed slice that has at
least 16 complete paired rows, reference mean score >= .90, at least four
positive and four nonpositive signed losses, and one variable pre-action
observable not already closed by 041, 045/046 or 047/048. These are eligibility
conditions, not tuning targets. No current slice is presumed to pass.

If no slice passes, record an honest impasse and stop collection under the
current Qwen/Knorm/NIAH, VT and MuSiQue line. The next owner decision must
change the compressor family or the task-quality substrate and define a new
decision-time observable before any new experiment. If a slice passes, it only
authorizes a fresh scoped design with action-aware matched baselines, grouped
splits, signed losses and a sealed unseen evaluation; it does not reopen any
closed result or open confirmation.

This audit is the smallest remaining step that can change the research
direction without spending GPU budget or weakening the expected signed
task-quality-loss objective. No external literature lookup is needed before
it; 053 already covers the bounded primary review.

## Owner assessment

Collection remains paused, but the proposed eligibility inventory is not selected. Existing audited records already establish the missing variation or closed observable families; the new16-row/.90 thresholds do not supply an independent scientific reason to revisit them. Requiring reference competence is a practical assay choice, not mathematical identifiability of signed loss. One bounded senior review will assess a genuinely different decision-time information mechanism before another GPU experiment. No new eligibility threshold, task, action, feature or model is selected by this note.
