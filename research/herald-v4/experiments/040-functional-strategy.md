# Decision after the fixed digit-copy probe

## Controlling evidence

Study038 failed its full predeclared gate. Correct-value damage separated the
outcome on the exposed outcome-stratified twelve cases, but the wrong-value
control had the same AUC, 0.889. Reference NLL had AUC 0.556. The exact semantic
probe is closed, and no prefix, reduction, or control may be revised on these
twelve outcomes. This does not erase the signal, but it changes its admissible
interpretation from queried-value retention to generic post-compression
continuation sensitivity.

## Competing hypotheses

1. **Generic functional sensitivity.** Knorm damage makes a cache broadly less
   able to sustain a high-probability multi-token continuation, and that state
   predicts final task loss. A task-independent, reference-supported probe
   should retain predictive value on fresh prompts and beat immediate
   distributional and structural baselines.

2. **Shared prompt-severity confounding.** Both sequences separate because some
   contexts suffer more global cache damage. The scalar may reproduce within
   this generator but add no value beyond global removal, prompt geometry, or
   immediate divergence on a blocked layout and wording population.

3. **Forced-prefix and digit-template artifact.** The shared answer prefix and
   seven-digit trajectory create the separation. A neutral, nonnumeric,
   task-independent probe will lose it on fresh cases even when signed losses
   remain variable.

4. **Real task-specific information is hidden by the control construction.**
   The wrong value's extremely unlikely first token and nearly certain forced
   suffix make its mean damage a poor specificity control. A prospectively
   defined task probe could beat a well-supported generic trajectory on new
   data, but this cannot be tested by revising reductions on the exposed twelve.

## Recommendation

Run one fresh frozen prediction study of **generic continuation sensitivity**.
Do not run another mechanism-only control on the exposed 96 prompts, and do not
describe study038 as a success. The new estimand remains signed final official
task-quality loss under the unchanged B0 native Knorm .10 action.

Before opening any new outcomes, freeze a task-independent probe: append one
fixed neutral cue, generate a short greedy reference trajectory from an
independent uncompressed cache, then score that exact trajectory under paired
reference and candidate-action caches. Use the mean reference-minus-action
token log probability as the sole new scalar. Pin cue, trajectory length,
position handling, and cost accounting without consulting labels. This removes
the known answer and digit parser while keeping the observed multi-token
functional idea. Treat the scalar as a candidate predictor, not as task-quality
loss or a causal mechanism.

Use entirely new prompt groups with blocked wording and fact-layout variants.
Collect a discovery split and keep an evaluation split locked. Require at least
six positive and six nonpositive outcomes in each split; otherwise the action
assay is uninformative and the study stops without fitting. Fit only a
one-dimensional calibrated predictor on discovery. Compare on locked evaluation
against the discovery mean, the existing structural/global-removal baseline,
reference NLL, and immediate boundary divergence. Preserve the prior gates:
at least 10 percent lower MSE and 32 of 48 prompt wins against every matched
baseline, plus AUC at least 0.80. Add no new features or subgroup decisions after
discovery.

If the generic predictor misses any original predictive gate, close this
functional-probe family and pivot to a new task/action population with less
saturated, more graded signed loss. Do not search another cue, prefix,
trajectory reduction, head subset, or model on these outcomes.

This is legitimate as a new hypothesis because its observable, interpretation,
population, baselines, and stopping rule are prospective. The equal control AUC
is the reason for the new hypothesis, not evidence that it has already passed.
